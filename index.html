Night Shift Lockdown

Time on the unit00:00

It is 0300. The unit’s badge system has locked down. Four doors stand between your team and the exit. Each door opens only when every answer in that room is correct. When a door opens, it shows one digit of the exit code.

If the lock stays shut, the panel marks which questions to recheck, but it will not tell you the answer. Stuck? Each question has a Where to look clue that points you to the slides or reading. Rationales for every question unlock after you escape.

These questions use new situations on purpose. They are harder than Exam 2, so you must recall each concept and apply it, not just recognize it.
